# Methodology and evidence limits

## Longitudinal inference

Time is elapsed calendar months (0, 2, 3, ..., 13), not campaign rank. AR(1) uses rho raised to the monthly distance. The Gaussian GEE mean contains group, time and interaction; seven responses use log(1+Y). Mancl-DeRouen standard errors and approximate t4 intervals replace conventional asymptotic inference. Interaction sensitivity uses t3. BH is applied to thirteen interaction tests per specification. With five stations, neither reference distribution guarantees accurate coverage. Shared campaign conditions can introduce additional dependence.

Slopes for transformed responses describe changes in Y+1; they are not percentage changes in the arithmetic mean of Y. The DO interaction has q=0.1188. A negative fitted Group A slope does not establish continuous decline; its nominal significance is sensitive to reference degrees of freedom.

## Multivariate and predictive analysis

EC is retained and redundant TDS/salinity excluded from multivariate models. PCA and clustering use eleven variables; DO prediction uses ten predictors. Substantial collinearity remains. Three PCs explain 85.6% descriptively; retention and resampling do not establish three stable environmental dimensions. The three-cluster solution is not uniquely stable.

Campaign-held-out validation retains all five observations of a campaign in the test fold. Scaling and nested selection use training data only. Fixed Elastic Net and nested family selection answer different evaluation questions and have separate scores. A training-mean comparator is evaluated on the identical test observations. Future forecasting and operational use require prospective independent validation, especially at low DO, represented by only three observations at or below 4 mg/L.

Groups A=P1-P3 and B=P4-P5 are predefined analytical memberships. Classification does not independently verify freshwater/marine influence. Geographic and hydrological evidence is still required.

## Anomalies and zero records

Consensus means at least two of three detectors. Main settings: contamination 0.10 for Isolation Forest and LOF, 15 LOF neighbors, robust-distance 0.90 quantile. Eighteen settings assess sensitivity. Flags prioritize archival investigation and do not establish pollution or measurement error. Full observations remain in the primary analysis.

One nitrite zero and two settleable-solids zeros are retained. Exclusion and hypothetical nitrite LOD/2 and LOQ/2 substitutions are separate sensitivity scenarios, not a correction of proven analytical censoring. Nitrite importance is sensitive to atypical profiles and correlated predictors; it is not a causal effect.

## Field and laboratory evidence

DO, pH and temperature were determined in the field; DO used Winkler determination. The laboratory BOD5 procedure began within 24 hours; incubation remains five days. Reported nitrate LOD/LOQ: 0.01/0.062 mg/L; nitrite: 0.000974/0.01 mg/L. TDS instrument: HACH Sension 156, reference solutions traceable to NIST; analytical blanks reported below detection limits.

Reported transport temperature of 10 degrees C requires confirmation. Instrument and container records, accreditation during sampling, duplicate/recovery data and limits for every parameter remain unavailable. Nitrogen species are retained in source units without imposing an ion-versus-nitrogen conversion. Settleable solids follow source mL/L; no unverified mass-volume conversion is performed. Arithmetic pH means are descriptive recorded-reading summaries, not the pH of a mixture; median and IQR provide complementary summaries.

## Historical scope

Thirteen campaigns spanning slightly more than one year cannot separate recurring seasonality from interval-specific trends. Historical comparison requires compatible stations, seasons, units and methods. Vences-Martínez et al. (2025) is part of the continuing research program, so an absolute absence of recent studies is not claimed.
